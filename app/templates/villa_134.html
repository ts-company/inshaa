About the project

One home, finished to a single standard

Villa 134 shows the quality we deliver. Every space in the house, from the reception hall to the maid's room, was designed and finished by our team as one complete project.

The house is built around a light palette of travertine, polished marble, natural oak and brushed brass. Joinery is made to measure throughout: open shelving in the living room, full-height wardrobes in the bedrooms, a library wall in the office and a walk-in closet in the master suite. Lighting is layered in every room, with recessed spots, hidden cove strips and statement pendants, so each space works by day and feels warm at night.

Across 550 m² on two floors and a roof level, set on a 600 m² plot, the villa holds a reception hall, a living room, a formal dining room, a main kitchen, a home office, a master suite, two children's bedrooms, a guest room, a maid's room, an upstairs kitchenette and a guest lounge on the roof.

Reception

An entrance hall built around the staircase

The front door opens into a double-height hall lined in travertine, with bronze-framed display shelving on either side. A floating stone staircase with a frameless glass balustrade rises through the house, and a cascade of hand-blown glass globes hangs through all three levels of the stairwell.

A slatted oak screen frames the stair and lets you see through to the living room beyond, so the hall feels open while still marking the arrival.

  • Travertine walls
  • Polished marble floor
  • Frameless glass balustrade
  • Glass globe chandelier
  • Oak slats

Living room

A long, bright room for the whole family

The living room runs the depth of the house, with floor-to-ceiling sheer curtains along the garden side. At one end, a travertine media wall sits between two bays of open oak shelving, each lit from within. A curved bouclé sofa, button-back lounge chairs and a cluster of bronze hexagonal tables make a second seating area closer to the stair.

Mirrored panels and slim bronze nesting tables keep the room feeling light, while the marble floor carries through from the hall without a break.

  • Travertine media wall
  • Oak shelving
  • Bronze side tables
  • Sheer linen curtains

Dining and kitchen

Formal dining and a working kitchen

The dining room seats ten at a glass-topped table under a long linear crystal pendant. Sculpted metallic mirrors and tall palms soften the room, and a walnut console holds serving pieces.

The main kitchen is made for everyday cooking. Handleless white cabinets run the full length of the room, with warm oak upper units and lighting hidden underneath. A waterfall island with bar stools sits in the middle, alongside a double-door refrigerator, built-in ovens and an induction hob.

  • Glass dining table
  • Linear crystal pendant
  • Waterfall island
  • Oak and white lacquer

Home office

A quiet study with a library wall

The office is set apart from the family rooms, with oak flooring, a dark executive desk and two curved guest chairs. The back wall is a full-height oak bookcase with LED strips set into every upright, and a brass linear pendant hangs over the desk.

A large abstract artwork and a fiddle-leaf fig give the room character without distracting from work.

  • Oak bookcase with LED
  • Brass pendant
  • Oak floor

Master suite

A private suite with its own closet and bath

The master bedroom is calm and soft: textured plaster walls, a padded headboard, oak bedside tables and faceted crystal pendants on each side of the bed. An oak-framed mirror reflects the room back on itself.

A walk-in closet lined with smoked-glass wardrobes leads to the bathroom, which has a floating oak double vanity, a whirlpool tub set on a teak deck under a lit cove, and a rain shower.

  • Textured plaster
  • Crystal pendants
  • Smoked-glass wardrobes
  • Whirlpool tub
  • Teak deck

Children's rooms

Two bedrooms made for growing up

Each child has a room shaped around their own taste. The daughter's room is in soft blush tones, with a quilted headboard, glass globe pendants, a study desk and a dressing table with a lit Hollywood mirror.

The son's room is in calm neutrals, with a full wall of built-in wardrobes, a desk under floating oak shelves and plenty of floor space to play. Both rooms have oak floors, sheer curtains and hidden cove lighting.

  • Built-in wardrobes
  • Study desks
  • Hollywood mirror
  • Oak floors

Guest room and roof lounge

Space for family and friends to stay

The guest room is warm and simple, with oak wardrobes with black bar handles, an oak-panelled wall behind the bed and a dressing table under a round mirror.

Up on the roof, the guest lounge has a slatted oak ceiling with warm cove lighting, a large screen and low seating, making a relaxed place to gather away from the main floors.

  • Oak wardrobes
  • Slatted oak ceiling
  • Cove lighting

Upstairs kitchenette

Breakfast without going downstairs

On the bedroom floor, a compact kitchenette has oak and white cabinets, a wine fridge and a round marble-topped table for four under a clear glass dome pendant. It opens onto the stair landing, so the family can share a quick breakfast or a late coffee close to the bedrooms.

The bathrooms throughout the house follow the same calm palette, with large-format stone-look tiles, glazed handmade-style wall tiles, built-in niches and matte black fittings.

Pool and garden

Pool, pergola and planted paths

The pool is finished in blue glass mosaic, with a built-in step and seating ledge for the children. A white pergola with timber slats shades a lounge on the travertine deck, and outdoor sofas and parasols sit along the water's edge.

Along the side of the house, stepping-stone paths run through lawn and dense planting under a row of timber portal frames with built-in lights, turning a service passage into a garden walk.

  • Glass mosaic pool
  • Travertine deck
  • Timber pergola
  • Lit timber portals
